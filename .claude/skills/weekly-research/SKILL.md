# Weekly Research

## Process
1. Read archive/topics-covered.md to see what's been covered recently.
2. Use web search to find what PMs and Designers are publicly discussing,
   debating, or frustrated about in the last 7 days.
3. Search sources: Reddit r/ProductManagement, r/UXDesign, LinkedIn
   public posts, X/Twitter, product management blogs, design community
   sites.
4. Use the talking-point-extractor skill to categorise findings into
   Educational, Spicy Take, Data Nugget, and Story Spark.
5. Identify 5–8 angles that are timely, opinionated, and relevant to the
   the company audience (PMs and Designers at web-first B2B SaaS companies).
   Avoid repeating topics from the last 4 weeks per topics-covered.md.
6. For each angle, capture:
   - Topic / angle name
   - Category (from talking-point-extractor)
   - Source URL(s)
   - Key insight (paraphrased, 2–3 sentences)
   - Why it matters to the target audience
   - Potential post framing (one sentence)

## Output
Save the signal file to output/research/YYYY-MM-DD-signals.md.
Append this week's angle names and dates to archive/topics-covered.md.

## Acceptance Criteria
- 5–8 distinct angles with verifiable sources
- Research window is strictly last 7 days
- Each angle categorised per talking-point-extractor framework
- No overlap with topics from last 4 weeks
- No fabricated sources or metrics
- Signal file saved to output/research/
- topics-covered.md updated
