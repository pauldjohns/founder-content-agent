Three teams told me the same thing last month: they stopped using the thing they built in the first
week.

Not because it broke. Because the person who asked for it had moved on to a different question by
the time it shipped, and nobody wanted to say so.

We keep treating that as a prioritisation problem. It isn't. It's a latency problem. If the gap
between "I want to know X" and "here is X" is three weeks, you are not answering questions, you are
answering the memory of questions.

The teams that got out of it did one unglamorous thing: they made the first version worse, on
purpose, and put it in front of the asker in two days.

---

mechanism rather than the moral, ends on a position instead of a question, and contains nothing that
could not be said out loud to a peer.
